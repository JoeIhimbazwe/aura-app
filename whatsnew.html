VERSION 0.2.2

A light that keeps time

  • NewBeat line. Two lights in the colour of the album run around the edges of your screen, and get bigger and brighter, with small waves, every time the beat kicks.
  • NewA living clock. Colours flow through the clock, and every few seconds it plays a new animation: the digits break in two, flip, bounce, split, zoom, swivel, glitch, spin or wipe. New minutes roll in, and it bumps with the beat.
  • NewClock fonts. Pick from 7 clock styles in the ··· menu: Condensed, Thin, Bold, Serif, Digital, Handwritten and Poster.
  • NewAlbums that never stop. When a song has no lyrics, the album cards come to the middle and slide by on their own, quickly and endlessly, with a light running around the album in front.
  • NewA calmer corner button. In wallpaper mode, the Aura logo sits quietly in the corner with a slow glow, and only while you're on your desktop. It steps away as soon as you open another app. Prefer more fun? Pick the pink neon sign in ··· › Corner button, or hide it.
  • BetterMusic videos now always play in HD.

VERSION 0.2.1

Lyrics that land on the beat

  • NewLyrics Timing. Right-click the lyrics and pick Lyrics Timing… Aura listens to the singer for a few seconds and lines the words up by itself, even switching to a better-matching version of the lyrics. It remembers every song. You can still nudge it by ±0.1s, for one song or all of them.
  • New28 lyric animations, from Neon, Glitch and Fire to Typewriter, Bounce and Rainbow, plus a Random mix. Each one plays a little preview before you choose.
  • NewBeat pops. The edge light now stays calm and shoots colourful peaks out of the screen edges every time the music hits.
  • BetterThe menu where you choose your music player now shows each player's own icon.
