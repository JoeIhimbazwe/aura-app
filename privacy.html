Privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Aura is a Windows app made by Jeff Ihimbazwe. This page explains what information Aura uses and why. In short: Aura collects as little as possible, never sells anything, and never asks for your name.

What Aura sends to our server

  • The title and artist of the song you're playing, so our server can find its music video. Matches are saved in a shared list (song → video) so each song only needs to be looked up once. This list doesn't say who played what.
  • An anonymous device code. This is a scrambled code made from your PC's Windows ID. It can't be turned back into the original ID and doesn't identify you. We use it for the 7-day free trial, to link a licence to your PC, and to share search limits fairly.
  • Your licence key, when you activate Aura, to check that it's valid.

Our server runs on Supabase. We don't use cookies, ads or tracking in the app.

What stays on your PC

  • Your settings, a list of songs and their videos (so replays are instant), and your Spotify login if you connect Spotify. These stay on your computer.
  • Aura reads what's playing from Windows' media controls and listens to your PC's sound output to make the edge light pulse. The sound is never recorded or sent anywhere, and Aura never uses your microphone.

YouTube

Aura uses YouTube API Services to find and play music videos. By using Aura you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Videos play through YouTube's own embedded player, which may use its own cookies. You can manage what Google can access in your Google security settings.

Spotify (optional)

If you choose to connect Spotify, you log in on Spotify's own website. Aura only gets permission to see and control what's playing and to start songs you pick. Your Spotify login is stored on your PC, not on our server. You can disconnect anytime in Aura's settings menu, or at spotify.com/account/apps.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Gumroad. We never see your card details. Gumroad's own privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Aura removes everything on your PC. To have your device code and licence removed from our server, contact us through our Gumroad page and include your licence key if you have one.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date.
